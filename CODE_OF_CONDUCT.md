# Code of Conduct

*Last Update: 2026-10-15*

1. Do not discriminate against or harass other people. Bigotry (including racism, homophobia, and transphobia) is not allowed. Affiliation with groups (political or otherwise) that espouse discriminatory beliefs is enough to be removed from the community by itself.
2. Try to avoid drama and keep conversations productive. Remember to discuss the idea and not the person who had it.
3. Keep all communication in English. Use of translation tools is allowed if you disclose their use.

## Generative AI Policy

**Anything made with the assistance of generative AI is not allowed** within this community as a whole. This includes, but is not limited to, any code created with the assistance of AI (even if the code itself was written by a human) and issues, code reviews, and comments made with the assistance of AI.

In addition to the usual concerns, please consider that use of AI is an immense violation of your own free will. It strips you of the ability to make decisions and think critically, and it forces others to interact with what is essentially nothing more than a canned parody of a human.
